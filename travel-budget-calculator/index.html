A quick estimate, not a trip planner

This calculator adds your approximate daily costs for the number of days entered, includes fixed costs, then applies the optional percentage buffer.

Use it to establish a practical starting budget. Actual prices, card fees and exchange rates can change.

How currency conversion works

The final destination-currency budget is converted with the latest available Frankfurter reference rate. The rate date and source appear with every result.

What to include

Accommodation, meals, local transport and activities are daily amounts. Add flights, rail passes or other one-time expenses under fixed trip costs.
