What the percentage means

The calculator first estimates how much the latest reference rate would produce. It then compares that amount with the amount you were offered.

A provider can advertise “no commission” while using a less favourable exchange rate. That shortfall is effectively a cost hidden in the rate.

Markup = (reference result − offered result) ÷ reference result × 100.

Important to know

This is an informational comparison, not financial advice. An offer may include service fees, card charges, cash handling, or a provider’s spread. Reference rates are not guaranteed transaction rates.
