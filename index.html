Made for real-world calculations wherever you are

BALCO Converter is a global conversion tool and currency calculator for travel, shopping and everyday price checks. Convert fiat currencies with updated reference exchange rates, then keep working offline with the last successfully saved rates.

Enter an amount in either currency field and switch direction whenever you need. The built-in calculator supports addition, subtraction, multiplication, division and percentages without leaving the converter.
