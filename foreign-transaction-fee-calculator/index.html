What Dynamic Currency Conversion means

DCC is a merchant or ATM offer to convert the transaction into your home currency before it reaches your card. Paying in local currency instead leaves the currency conversion to your card provider.

The calculator compares the complete DCC amount you enter with an estimate based on the reference rate, your percentage foreign transaction fee, and your fixed fee.

What this estimate cannot know

Your bank, card network, merchant or ATM may use a different exchange rate or charge another fee you did not enter. A fixed ATM fee is separate from a percentage foreign transaction fee.

This is an informational comparison of the values entered, not financial advice or a promise of the final amount charged.

A different comparison?

Use the Exchange Rate Markup Calculator when you simply want to compare an exchange quote with the reference result. Use the Currency Converter for a direct two-currency estimate.

Reference rate and date

Reference rates provide a neutral comparison point, not a guaranteed transaction rate. The result shows the market date returned by the Frankfurter reference-rate API.
