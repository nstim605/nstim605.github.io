Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 1, 2026

Balkan Converter respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information is used when you use the application.

Information We Collect

Balkan Converter does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, precise location, contacts, photos, microphone data, or other personal information during normal use.

Exchange Rate Data

Exchange rates are retrieved from the Frankfurter API, which uses reference data published by the European Central Bank (ECB). Standard technical information, such as an IP address, may be processed as part of a normal network request. Balkan Converter does not use this information to identify or track users.

Local Data

Application settings, conversion history, theme preferences, and other preferences are stored locally on your device. This information is not sent to the developer.

Contacting the Developer

If you choose to contact the developer, you may voluntarily provide your email address and message. Your message is sent through your chosen email application. Balkan Converter does not send emails directly and does not store your messages on developer-operated servers.

Advertising

Balkan Converter may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and process certain information to provide, personalize, measure, and improve advertising services.

For more information, please see Google's Privacy Policy:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

Payments

The current version does not process payments. If paid features, such as an option to remove advertisements, are introduced in the future, payments will be handled through Google Play, and this Privacy Policy will be updated where necessary.

Data Retention

Locally stored settings and conversion history remain on your device until you clear them, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Children

Balkan Converter is a general-purpose currency utility and is not specifically directed at children.

Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's functionality changes. The latest version will always be available within the application and on this page.

Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please use the Contact Developer form available within Balkan Converter.
