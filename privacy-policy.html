Privacy Policy

2026-10-21

BALCO Converter is provided by TimPixelLab. No app account is required. The app does not access contacts, precise location or the microphone. Its currency calculations and history are local; the network and SDK processing described below are separate.

Exchange-rate requests go to Frankfurter; cryptocurrency requests go to CoinGecko or Kraken over HTTPS. Providers receive network information such as an IP address and requested currencies, coin identifiers or dates, but not entered conversion amounts, saved-set names, history or search text. Rates and catalogs are cached for offline use.

The price scanner uses the camera and bundled Google ML Kit to recognize text on the device. BALCO does not upload or save camera images; selected text from another app is also parsed locally. ML Kit separately sends technical telemetry to Google, including device/app information, installation identifiers, processing time, configuration, input/output sizes and errors. This SDK telemetry is not controlled by the Usage analytics switch. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure.

Google Mobile Ads provides a banner and optional rewarded ads. A rewarded ad starts only when requested and grants 20 BALCO Points (BP) only after the SDK confirms the reward. Verified BALCO Plus removes ads. Google may collect and share IP-derived approximate location, device/app identifiers, interactions, diagnostics and consent signals for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. UMP manages applicable advertising consent and Privacy Options. These choices are separate from Usage analytics. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

Google Analytics for Firebase stays off until you opt in to Usage analytics; refusing does not limit functionality. After opt-in it processes app-instance identifiers, device/app context, approximate geography, sessions, screen activity and feature events, including Plus purchase and restoration events. Store integrations may also report product information and purchase prices. BALCO does not send conversion amounts, currency pairs, history, saved sets, scanned or selected text in its analytics events. No developer-defined User-ID is set, and Analytics advertising-ID collection is disabled. You can turn Usage analytics off in Settings; this stops future collection and resets local analytics state, but does not delete previously transmitted or aggregated data. Provider retention depends on their terms and service settings. The linked Google Analytics and AdMob services exchange analytics data, including purchase-related metrics, for monetization reporting and to improve advertising services.

Payments are handled by the app store, not by BALCO. In the Google Play version, BALCO Plus has monthly and yearly auto-renewing plans; you manage or cancel them in Google Play. Existing Scanner Unlimited ownership can be restored. To grant and restore access, BALCO processes product IDs, purchase states, acknowledgment status and purchase tokens through the store SDK. It does not receive card or bank details or send purchase tokens to a developer-operated server or its analytics events. Pending acknowledgment tokens are stored privately until processed. Plus access is rechecked with the store. BP balances, recent BP transactions, reward-deduplication identifiers, theme unlocks and daily free-scan usage are stored locally.

Android backup and device transfer exclude the main preferences (including conversion history, favorites, saved sets and general settings), cached Plus entitlement, pending acknowledgment tokens and Firebase consent/queued analytics. BP-related data, unlocked themes, daily scanner allowance, cryptocurrency preferences/cache and widget settings may be backed up or transferred by Android, depending on device settings. There is no BALCO account or developer-operated cloud sync; restoration is not guaranteed. Local data remains until cleared or the app is uninstalled; Android backups and provider records may remain separately. Clearing app data or uninstalling does not cancel a store subscription.

Contact developer opens your chosen email app with your email/message and app/Android version and device model. Sending is your choice; the developer receives what you send and uses it to answer support requests. For privacy questions or requests, contact nstim605@gmail.com; some data is controlled by Google or other providers. Google Play reviews do not reveal a rating, review text or submission confirmation to BALCO. Reviewed network transfers use HTTPS/TLS, not end-to-end encryption. BALCO is a general-purpose utility, not specifically directed at children. This policy may change when the app or its services change.

Website privacy

balkanconverter.com uses optional Firebase/Google Analytics only after Accept analytics. It measures pages, traffic sources/campaign tags, browser/device context and interactions such as Google Play link clicks. Google receives technical request information and may use first-party identifiers or cookies. Reject analytics prevents loading these libraries. Your choice is stored in your browser; change it through Analytics choices in the footer or clear site data. This analytics integration denies advertising storage, advertising user data and advertising personalization. Local/QA traffic is excluded when the supported QA marker is present.

The website separately uses Google AdSense. Google and advertising partners may process technical requests, consent, ad interactions, approximate location and cookies or similar storage where permitted. Applicable consent messages let you refuse or manage advertising purposes without losing access to the site; advertising choices are separate from optional analytics. Hosting and rate providers may receive request/IP information. See https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.
