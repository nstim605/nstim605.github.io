ECB raises its three key interest rates by 25 basis points

The ECB raised its three key rates by 25 basis points, including the deposit facility rate to 2.50%, effective 16 September 2026.

Published 10 September 2026 · Updated 11 September 2026

🇪🇺 EUR

Summary

The European Central Bank increased all three key interest rates by 0.25 percentage points. The new rates took effect on 16 September 2026.

What happened

The deposit facility rate was set at 2.50%, the main refinancing operations rate at 2.65%, and the marginal lending facility rate at 2.90%. The ECB said inflation was expected to remain above its medium-term target for an extended period and that future decisions would remain data-dependent.

Why it matters

ECB policy rates influence euro-area borrowing conditions and expectations about monetary policy. That makes the decision directly relevant when comparing or converting amounts involving the euro.
