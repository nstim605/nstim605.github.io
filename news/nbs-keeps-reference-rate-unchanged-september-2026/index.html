National Bank of Serbia keeps its reference rate at 5.75%

The National Bank of Serbia left its key policy rate unchanged at 5.75% at the 10 September 2026 meeting.

Published 10 September 2026

🇷🇸 RSD

Summary

The Executive Board of the National Bank of Serbia kept the reference interest rate at 5.75% on 10 September 2026.

What happened

The published 2026 decision record shows no change in the reference rate at the September meeting. The rate had remained at 5.75% at each scheduled meeting from January through September.

Why it matters

The NBS reference rate is an important signal for dinar borrowing conditions and monetary policy in Serbia. It provides useful context when comparing prices or converting amounts involving the Serbian dinar.
